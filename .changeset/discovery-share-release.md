On-demand share release round for threshold-shared group secrets. A group member asks the share holders for their shares when it needs the secret (`requestShareRelease`), instead of only holding shares dealt up front. A holder (`createShareHolder`, over the protocol channel named by `stealthReleaseChannel(agentId)`, `stealth:<agentId>:share:release`) answers only members of its roster: the request is signed by the member, bound to one holder, a time and a nonce, and names the agent only by a hash tag. A holder checks size and version, addressee and freshness, a per-requester rate limit, the signature, a replay cache, membership (before it looks at the tag, so a non-member learns nothing about what it holds), the epoch floor (`minEpoch`, `retire()`), and an optional `approve` hook; it answers with its signed share sealed to the requester (X25519, HKDF, AES-256-GCM) and signed by the holder, or a named refusal (`RELEASE_REFUSALS`). The reader verifies every share against the dealer, `minEpoch` and `holders`, stops at the threshold, and reports released, refused and unreachable holders; fewer than `t` valid shares throws `below-threshold` with those counts. Shares and the existing threshold module are unchanged (`v: 1`), the messages are new, and a peer that does not run the channel is simply unreachable. Off by default: nothing sends or answers a release request unless the application wires it. New exports: `createShareHolder`, `requestShareRelease`, `createReleaseRequest`, `createReleaseResponse`, `stealthReleaseChannel`, `stealthReleaseTag`, `RELEASE_REFUSALS`.
