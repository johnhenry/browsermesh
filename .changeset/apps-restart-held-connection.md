Two gaps around a peer that starts over on a WebRTC connection this node already holds. First, a peer that went away without a close this side had seen yet (its channel closing, or its state failed) left a connection that was held and handed to the node but not open, and the negotiator told the node `restarted` only for an open one: the fresh offer swapped the connection underneath, nothing reported the old one closed, and the channel link over it stayed up for a node that remembers nothing of it. A connection is now held when it is open or was handed to the node and has not closed since, and a renegotiation (an ICE restart, whose answer says so) keeps the channel and what runs over it and is no longer reported as a restart. Second, a peer that speaks the moment the connection is up (the opening frame of a channel it opens at once, as a running ingress does after a restart) could be heard by nobody, because the node listens to the connection only once it has built the session around it, a little after the connection opens: its opening frame was dropped and the channel waited out its open timeout. The adapter the negotiator hands out now holds what arrives (up to 64 frames) until its first listener and gives it to that listener in order. Nothing is added to the wire and no option or default changes: an older peer is served as before.
